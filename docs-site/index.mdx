Corely is a collection of connected, AI-native apps for running a team’s internal operations. Teams start with the capabilities they need and expand into finance and specialist operations as their work demands.

Finance is optional. Accounting is not a prerequisite for operational apps. Capabilities shown depend on your workspace configuration and permissions. The proposed operational package is still being validated; the long-term app map is not a list of available features.

[Corely product strategy](https://github.com/Corely-AI/corely/blob/development/docs/products/corely/README.md).

Welcome to Corely Docs.

## What you can do with Corely

- Manage clients, invoices, and expenses
- Automate workflows with the Assistant
- Keep everything consistent across your team
